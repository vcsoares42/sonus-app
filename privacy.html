Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

At Sonus, we value your privacy. This app was built to work without sign-ups, without accounts, and without identifying who uses it. Sonus has no server of its own: the sounds ship inside the app, and what you set up stays on your device.

What we collect

The app does not ask for sign-ups, email, name, phone number, location, or financial data, does not use the microphone, and shows no advertising. To understand how the app is used and improve it, we collect anonymous usage data: which screens were opened, which sounds and mixes were played and for how long, how the sleep timer was set, whether the bedtime reminder is on, whether the app was opened from the widget or the lock screen, the app language and version, the iOS version and device model, and whether a subscription was viewed, started or completed.

This data is tied only to a random identifier generated on your device the first time the app opens, unrelated to you, your Apple account, your email, or the iOS advertising identifier. When this data is received, the analytics service uses the connection's IP address to estimate the country and region of use; we do not collect your precise location. We do not record your screen, do not track you across apps, and do not sell this data.

You can turn usage data off at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share Anonymous Usage Data. The app keeps working normally with it off.

Data stored only on your device

Some information is saved only on your device so the app can work, and it never leaves it:

  • the mixes you saved, with their name, sounds and per-layer volumes;
  • your sleep timer settings and the bedtime reminder time;
  • your preferences for appearance, resuming sound when the app opens, and sharing usage data;
  • what is currently playing, shared with the home screen widget on the device itself.

When you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Notifications

The bedtime reminder uses local notifications, scheduled on the device itself. No information is sent to servers for this.

Widget, lock screen and Siri

Playback controls in the widget, on the lock screen and through Siri shortcuts work entirely on the device, with no internet access.

Subscriptions and payments

Sonus Pro is sold entirely by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment data. Managing and canceling subscriptions is done in your Apple account settings, and the handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy. To validate the purchase, its status (active, in free trial, canceled, lifetime) is processed by the RevenueCat service, tied only to the same random device identifier. This processing is required to unlock what you bought and happens even with usage data sharing turned off.

Services we use

The data described above is processed by two services that act on our behalf and do not use it for any other purpose:

  • PostHog, for usage statistics, with servers in the United States;
  • RevenueCat, for subscription status.

We do not sell any data to third parties or use it for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with the update date at the top.

Your rights and contact
